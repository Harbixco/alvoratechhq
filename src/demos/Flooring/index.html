ABOUT ALVORATECH

Architectural Precision.
Crafted From The Foundation Up.

At Alvoratech, we know that breathtaking floors begin beneath the surface. While typical contractors rush to lay planks over irregular subfloors, our master installers conduct comprehensive digital moisture profiling, laser grade leveling, and acoustic decoupling to ensure your floors remain dead flat, quiet, and stunning for decades.

Whether you are curating European wide-plank white oak for a custom residence, upgrading to 100% waterproof rigid core luxury vinyl plank (LVP), or reviving historical hardwoods with dustless restoration, we handle furniture moving, existing floor haul-away, and final trim work seamlessly.

Precision Subfloor Preparation

Self-leveling underlayment and digital planar mapping guarantee the NWFA standard of 3/16" flatness across a 10-foot radius.

Digital Moisture Profiling

Pinless capacitive moisture testing across concrete and plywood prevents cupping, crowning, and post-installation plank failure.

White-Glove Furniture & Clean Service

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about acclimatization, subfloor leveling, dustless sanding, and our lifetime installation warranty.

Solid and engineered hardwood planks typically require 48 to 72 hours of indoor acclimatization with your HVAC running at normal living temperatures (65°–75°F and 35–55% relative humidity). Our installers measure moisture content in both the wood and subfloor to ensure the moisture differential is under 2% to 4% before installing.

Yes. Our rigid core luxury vinyl planks (SPC) are 100% waterproof from top to bottom. Even if exposed to standing water from a plumbing leak or basement condensation, the planks will not swell, buckle, warp, or delaminate.

Our truck-mounted vacuum and portable two-stage HEPA containment systems capture 99.8% of airborne dust right at the machine head. You do not need to cover furniture or clean air ducts after we finish. We also use premium low-VOC waterborne finishes that cure quickly with virtually no toxic fumes.

Yes. We offer complete white-glove service. Our crews move standard household furniture, tear out old carpet, tack strips, or tile, dispose of all debris, vacuum the subfloor, and return your furniture to its exact place once the installation is complete.

Solid hardwood is a single piece of 3/4" milled timber, ideal for above-grade rooms and able to be refinished 6 to 8 times. Engineered hardwood features a real sawn timber top layer over cross-laminated birch or eucalyptus plywood, providing superior dimensional stability against humidity fluctuations and allowing wider plank formats (up to 9").

If a subfloor has dips or humps exceeding 3/16", wide-plank click-lock systems will flex when walked on, leading to cracked joints, hollow footstep sounds, and eventual plank separation. We grind high spots and pour self-leveling cement to guarantee a dead-flat foundation.
