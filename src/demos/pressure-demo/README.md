# ALVORATECH – Premium Pressure Washing & Exterior Cleaning Website

A custom-designed, conversion-optimized, responsive website for **ALVORATECH**, an American pressure washing and exterior cleaning company. Features interactive before-and-after transformation comparisons, 8 specialized service modules, an interactive photo gallery with lightbox, and direct WhatsApp quote request integration.

   2. House Washing

6. **Interactive Before-and-After Transformation Slider**:
   - 5 selectable surface transformations: Driveway, Concrete Walkway, Patio, House Siding, Wooden Deck.
   - Draggable slider handle with BEFORE / AFTER labels. Supports mouse drag, click, and mobile touch swipe.
   - Clearly labeled illustrative demonstration content.

   - 4-step timeline: Get in Touch → Discuss Your Property → Request Your Quote → Enjoy a Fresher Property.
8. **Our Work Gallery with Lightbox**:
   - 8 high-resolution photos categorized by surface type with interactive category filters and keyboard-navigable modal lightbox.
9. **Customer Testimonials Carousel**:
   - Sample demonstration reviews with 5-star ratings, touch swipe, auto-slide, pause on hover, and pagination dots.
10. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**:
    - 7 questions addressing pressure vs. soft washing, roof safety, surface types, and quote requests.
11. **Lead Generation Contact Form**:
    - Validation for name, email, phone, service required, property type, and message.
    - Encodes all fields and automatically opens a formatted inquiry in WhatsApp.
