Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct, honest answers regarding our exterior surface pressure washing and soft washing solutions.

Pressure washing is highly effective for durable hard surfaces including concrete driveways, stone patios, pavers, brick walkways, curbs, parking lots, and exterior building hardscapes. For softer surfaces, we utilize low-pressure soft washing techniques.

Pressure washing uses high-velocity water streams to dislodge tough stains and grime from durable surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to safely sanitize delicate surfaces like siding and roofs without causing mechanical damage.

Not every exterior material should receive high-pressure water. Vinyl siding, delicate wood, stucco, and shingles require gentle soft washing to prevent water intrusion or surface degradation. We always select the appropriate method for each material.
