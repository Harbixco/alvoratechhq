## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Three Distinct Demo Banners**:
   - **Top Demo Banner**: Dismissible blue banner notifying visitors of the demonstration status.
   - **Middle Demo Banner**: Highlight card with direct WhatsApp link.
   - **Bottom Demo Banner**: Pre-footer call-to-action banner.
2. **Fixed Floating WhatsApp Banner**:
   - Anchored to the bottom-right viewport across all devices.
   - Subtle pulse and hover interactions.
   - Direct link to `+1 (412) 796-3968` via WhatsApp URL.
3. **Hero Section**:
   - High-impact split layout with high-resolution photography.
   - Dual CTAs: *"Get a Free Quote"* and *"Explore Our Services"*.
   - Floating glass cards showcasing inspection standards and satisfaction focus.
4. **Trust Indicators Bar**:
   - 4 core value pillars: Professional Cleaning, Attention to Detail, Flexible Scheduling, and Customer-Focused Service.
5. **Services Grid**:
   - 6 specialized cleaning cards:
     - Residential Cleaning
     - Deep Cleaning
     - Commercial Cleaning
     - Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
     - Kitchen & Bathroom Cleaning
     - General Maintenance Cleaning
   - Each card features a direct WhatsApp service request link with a pre-filled service inquiry.
6. **Why Choose Alvoratech**:
   - Highlight of 6 core advantages with custom SVG iconography.
7. **How It Works Timeline**:
   - 4-step process from initial contact to enjoying a fresh space.
8. **Interactive Gallery & Lightbox**:
   - Category filtering (All, Residential, Kitchen & Bath, Commercial).
   - Clickable lightbox with keyboard arrow and ESC navigation.
9. **Customer Testimonials Carousel**:
   - Labeled demonstration reviews with star ratings.
   - Vanilla JS slider with auto-rotation, touch swipe, prev/next controls, and pagination dots.
10. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**:
    - Smooth expand/collapse animation with `aria-expanded` attributes.
11. **Client Request Form**:
    - Full client-side validation for required fields, email format, and phone number.
    - Encodes submitted values directly into a formatted WhatsApp inquiry and opens WhatsApp in a new tab.
