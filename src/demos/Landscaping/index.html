We excavate to virgin subsoil, lay commercial-grade woven geotextile fabric to prevent subgrade contamination, compact dense crushed aggregate stone base in 2-inch lifts with heavy vibratory compactors, screed bedding sand, and secure all edges with heavy-duty concrete bond beams and polymeric locking joint sand.
