WHY OUR COATINGS OUTLAST OTHERS

Our 5-Step Meticulous Preparation Process

90% of paint failures stem from rushed preparation. At Alvoratech, we spend twice as much time prepping surfaces as we do applying paint.

01

Total Shielding & Masking

Every floor, furnishing, fixture, and window is completely sealed using clean builder paper, static-cling plastic, and delicate-surface tape.

02

HEPA Dustless Sanding

We sand every surface with Festool HEPA extractors, eliminating 99.9% of airborne particles while smoothing out blemishes and prior brush marks.

03

Caulking & Seamless Patch

All baseboard seams, window casing gaps, and trim joints are filled with 50-year elastomeric acrylic caulk to stop air drafts and shadow lines.

04

High-Adhesion Stain Primer

We apply high-bonding, stain-blocking primers over raw wood, repairs, or tannins to guarantee flawless adhesion and eliminate bleed-through.

05

Dual Premium Finish Coats

Two generous coats of architectural-grade enamel or acrylic are applied with airless sprayers or fine microfiber rollers for uniform depth.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our painting timelines, surface preparation, paint grades, and warranties.

An average single or two-room interior refresh typically takes 1 to 2 days. A complete whole-home interior generally requires 3 to 5 business days, and complete home exteriors take 3 to 6 days depending on weather and structural prep. Your written proposal includes an exact timeline guarantee.

We ask only that you clear small personal knickknacks, fragile decor, and electronics from the rooms. Our crew will carefully move all large furniture to the center of the room and wrap it securely under heavy-duty plastic sheeting and clean drop cloths.

We work exclusively with America’s premier architectural coatings, predominantly Sherwin-Williams (Duration®, Emerald®, SuperPaint®) and Benjamin Moore (Aura®, Regal® Select, Advance® Cabinet Finish). We do not use watered-down builder-grade paints.

We remove all cabinet doors and drawer fronts to be professionally prepped, degreased, sanded, primed, and sprayed off-site in our controlled spray booth with conversion urethane enamel. Meanwhile, your cabinet frames in the kitchen are masked, prepped, and sprayed on-site for a factory-level finish.

Yes. All of our interior wall and ceiling coatings are zero-VOC or ultra-low-VOC formulations that emit virtually zero odor. You and your family can sleep safely in your home the very same night without unpleasant fumes or chemical smells.

Our 2-year warranty covers any peeling, chipping, flaking, or blistering caused by coating application defects or surface preparation failure. If an issue occurs, our team will promptly inspect and repair the affected area at zero cost to you.
